## NV-17-01

**Outcome / scope:** Split/bonus factor series and anchored adjusted views with unchanged raw facts.

**Requirements:** RES-02. **Dependencies:** Phase prerequisites above and the approved baseline.

### Acceptance

- **NV-17-01-AC1:** Split/bonus adjustment uses declared anchor/factor direction and preserves original raw prices, quantities and action evidence.
- **NV-17-01-AC2:** Compatible adjusted price×volume invariants hold within explicit tolerances; unsupported economic-action periods remain flagged.
- **NV-17-01-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

### Verification design

Check hand-calculated prices and share volumes strictly before, on and after the ex-date under admissible decision/anchor choices. Include multiple actions, unknown terms, zero/negative factors and ambiguous same-day ordering; compare raw storage hashes before/after computation.

## NV-17-04

**Outcome / scope:** Implement calendar-aware weekly/monthly aggregation of stored EOD through shared pure recipes, with field-specific reducers, gap/partial-period policy and correction lineage.

**Requirements:** RES-02 RES-01 TIME-01. **Dependencies:** `NV-17-03` plus phase prerequisites; use sequential internal checkpoints as needed; no separate execution-plan files.

### Acceptance

- **NV-17-04-AC1:** Weekly Monday–Sunday and calendar-month aggregation uses the evidenced session calendar and field-specific reducers; first/last OHLC, extrema, additive quantities and last OI match independent expectations without crossing incompatible identities/bases.
- **NV-17-04-AC2:** Incomplete/gapped periods and unknown sessions remain explicit; default excludes unfinished periods, availability is no earlier than required inputs, and corrections recompute affected bars with pinned lineage.
- **NV-17-04-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

### Verification design

Hand-calculate a holiday-shortened week, month boundary, special session, missing session, close-only series and contract/series transition. Test partial-period opt-in, adjustment basis, delivery ratio from compatible quantities and correction recomputation; assert no fetch or forward-fill.
