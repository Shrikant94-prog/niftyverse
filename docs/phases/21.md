## NV-21-01

**Outcome / scope:** Safe bounded MIME cards/sparklines and notebook build events/cancellation.

**Requirements:** UX-02 API-01. **Dependencies:** Phase prerequisites above and the approved baseline.

### Acceptance

- **NV-21-01-AC1:** MIME cards/sparklines are bounded, escaped, useful offline and fall back when optional extras are missing.
- **NV-21-01-AC2:** Display triggers no network/query/full materialization; notebook build cancellation reports actual builder state.
- **NV-21-01-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

### Verification design

Test malicious labels, long strings, decimal/null/timezones, zero/one/large result metadata and saved static fallback. Spy on I/O and measure serialized payload bounds.

<a id="nv-21-02"></a>
## NV-21-02

**Outcome / scope:** Snapshot-bound filtering/pagination, owned-query interruption and worker cleanup on cancel/close, stale-callback rejection and provenance details. Expose fact-to-document/context and derived-input trace details without implicit fetching; direct-result previews work without a store.

**Requirements:** UX-02 API-01. **Dependencies:** `NV-21-01` plus applicable prerequisite contracts. Independent work may continue only under the phase dependency rule above.

### Acceptance

- **NV-21-02-AC1:** Explorer filtering/pagination queries the chosen immutable snapshot with bounded results and independent query ownership.
- **NV-21-02-AC2:** Cancel/close interrupts and cleans up its query/worker/connection; late stale callbacks cannot overwrite a newer result or touch disposed widgets.
- **NV-21-02-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

- **NV-21-02-AC4:** Direct results have bounded offline previews and fact trace; inspection never downloads a missing document or implies unknown source/timing evidence (AM-17).

### Verification design

Use delayed overlapping queries with deterministic completion order, cancel and close at each boundary. Verify snapshot IDs, resource cleanup and rejection of stale responses.
