## NV-05-01

**Outcome / scope:** Stable issuer/instrument/listing IDs, dated aliases and versioned listing/series policy; reject ambiguous resolution. Establish stable derivative identity versus dated-term schema for Phase 10. Include ETF identity and local catalogue search with dated filters, explicit ambiguity and stale/current-only evidence.

**Requirements:** ID-01 TIME-01. **Dependencies:** Phase prerequisites above and the approved baseline.

### Acceptance

- **NV-05-01-AC1:** Renamed/reused symbols resolve to dated stable IDs, and ambiguous overlapping mappings fail visibly rather than joining unrelated securities.
- **NV-05-01-AC2:** EQ → BE → EQ history and derivative stable identity versus dated terms have explicit effective/known-at policies; missing dates remain unknown.
- **NV-05-01-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.
- **NV-05-01-AC4:** The additive `market_state_observations` companion schema preserves overlapping state kinds, source rule/band basis, nullable bounds and effective/known-at evidence. Observed presence, explicit removal, unknown coverage and not-applicable remain distinct; a snapshot does not invent a validity interval (AM-01).

- **NV-05-01-AC5:** Local catalogue search returns dated ETF/equity identities and all ambiguous matches with stale/current-only limits; no first-hit resolution or network on search (AM-15/AM-16).

### Verification design

Test interval boundaries, same symbol/different issuers, overlapping aliases and series transitions against hand-authored timelines. Assert one intended identity or an explicit unresolved result. Include overlapping ASM/GSM/band observations, incomplete-list absence and explicit removal; no missing historical state becomes unrestricted by default.
