## NV-16-01

**Outcome / scope:** Evidence policies, version eligibility and temporal selection before revision ranking.

**Requirements:** TIME-01 RES-01. **Dependencies:** Phase prerequisites above and the approved baseline.

### Acceptance

- **NV-16-01-AC1:** Eligibility by effective/availability evidence is evaluated before revision ranking; future revisions and unknown precision cannot leak into strict decisions.
- **NV-16-01-AC2:** Strict, locally observed, latest and declared-lag modes are distinguishable with recorded evidence/policy identity.
- **NV-16-01-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.
- **NV-16-01-AC4:** Surveillance states and quality/anomaly resolutions select only admissible versions. Missing explicitly required state coverage blocks that workflow; optional unknowns and anomaly exclusions retain eligible rows with reasons. Later explanations cannot silently change earlier eligibility (AM-01/AM-02).

### Verification design

Use independent timeline tables for original/revised/withdrawn/reinstated values, exact cutoff equality and date-only uncertainty; assert selected occurrence or explicit missing reason. Include overlapping regulatory states, a current-only list, a genuine crash and a later anomaly resolution under strict and retrospective policies.
