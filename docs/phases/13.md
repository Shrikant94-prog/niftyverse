## Phase integration gate — PH-13-GATE

quarter/annual/YTD and consolidated/standalone separation; revenue subtraction allowed only when valid, EPS/ratio/instant-balance subtraction rejected; cross-filing restatement does not duplicate facts or confuse newest period; withdrawal/reinstatement and missing facts remain explicit. P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and segments retain evidenced context/unit/period distinctions; each supported family has independent real-filing and held-out evidence.

<a id="nv-13-01"></a>
## NV-13-01

**Outcome / scope:** Legacy/integrated catalog generations and revision deduplication preserving provenance.

**Requirements:** FUND-01 TIME-01. **Dependencies:** Phase prerequisites above and the approved baseline.

### Acceptance

- **NV-13-01-AC1:** Legacy/integrated filing catalogs map to stable filing identities while retaining source occurrences and publication precision.
- **NV-13-01-AC2:** Deduplication does not discard a genuine restatement or withdrawal and cannot turn catalog-only coverage into numerical facts.
- **NV-13-01-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

### Verification design

Replay duplicate/original/revised/withdrawn catalog records across generations; assert identity, state and explicit no-facts status.

<a id="nv-13-02"></a>
## NV-13-02

**Outcome / scope:** Versioned economic mappings from JSON, XBRL/inline-XBRL and registered legacy financial HTML; units, context/dimensions, accounting basis, period/additivity against the evolving capability manifest.

**Requirements:** FUND-01. **Dependencies:** `NV-13-01` plus applicable prerequisite contracts. Independent work may continue only under the phase dependency rule above.

### Acceptance

- **NV-13-02-AC1:** Each minimum-manifest concept declares units, accounting basis, instant/duration/additivity and period semantics; quarter/YTD/annual and standalone/consolidated remain distinct.
- **NV-13-02-AC2:** Missing or unverified detail concepts remain explicitly unsupported; zero, null and unit scale are not interchangeable.
- **NV-13-02-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

- **NV-13-02-AC4:** XML/inline-XBRL/registered HTML mappings retain namespaces, dimensions, source scale/precision and unmapped assertions; no filename/context-name/blanket-crore assumptions. Each supported family has independent real-filing numerical evidence (AM-14).

### Verification design

Check verified concept samples against independent expected values and dictionary contracts, including monetary scale, fiscal-period and missing-concept cases.

<a id="nv-13-03"></a>
## NV-13-03

**Outcome / scope:** Restricted additive-period derivations, reported/derived precedence and cross-filing analytical selection; original/restated comparative tests, coverage and catalog-only diagnostics.

**Requirements:** FUND-01 TIME-01. **Dependencies:** `NV-13-02` plus applicable prerequisite contracts. Independent work may continue only under the phase dependency rule above.

### Acceptance

- **NV-13-03-AC1:** Compatible additive revenue may derive a quarter; EPS, ratios and instant balances reject generic subtraction with a clear reason.
- **NV-13-03-AC2:** A later restated Q1 comparative changes admissible Q1 selection without duplicating analytical keys or making Q1 the newest period over Q2; reported/derived precedence is explicit.
- **NV-13-03-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

### Verification design

Use hand-calculated original/Q2 comparative/restated/withdrawn/reinstated timelines. Assert one fact per analytical key, correct latest-period choice and rejection of nonadditive derivations.

<a id="nv-13-04"></a>
## NV-13-04

**Outcome / scope:** Expose P&L, balance-sheet, cash-flow and segment views over normalized financial facts, with statement/context coverage, traceable missingness and expanded held-out real-filing checks.

**Requirements:** FUND-01 TIME-01. **Dependencies:** `NV-13-03` plus phase prerequisites; use sequential internal checkpoints as needed; no separate execution-plan files.

### Acceptance

- **NV-13-04-AC1:** All four statement views preserve exact period/basis/unit/dimensions and reported-versus-derived lineage. Segment rows remain distinct and an absent statement is reported, not zero-filled.
- **NV-13-04-AC2:** Each claimed supported family maps independently checked real facts and passes held-out samples; decoder success, unmapped concepts and validated coverage remain separate statuses without a universal earliest-year claim.
- **NV-13-04-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

### Verification design

Use industrial/banking or other sector families as each mapper is implemented, with instant balance sheets, duration cash flows, segments, comparative/restated facts and varied bases. Freeze independent expected decimals and locators. Expand issuer/year tests incrementally and publish gaps rather than inferring continuity from two endpoints.
