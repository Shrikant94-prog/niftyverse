<a id="nv-04-01"></a>
## NV-04-01

**Outcome / scope:** Define typed schemas/migrations, direct-raw/SDK capture envelopes, assertion/content/check identities, withdrawal states, analytical fact keys, partition contracts and writer lock. Include filing documents/source assertions, parser/taxonomy identities, ETF schemas and a common validated-batch contract for DB/file sinks.

**Requirements:** STORE-01 RES-03. **Dependencies:** Phase prerequisites above and the approved baseline.

### Acceptance

- **NV-04-01-AC1:** Typed migrations preserve documented keys/types and old readable metadata; content identity, assertion occurrence, source check and withdrawal are distinct.
- **NV-04-01-AC2:** Only one owning process can write; partition scope/field/basis and analytical fact keys cannot collapse incompatible observations.
- **NV-04-01-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.
- **NV-04-01-AC4:** Store defaults resolve to OS-local nonsynchronized storage for the catalog, WAL, locks, raw objects, staging and spill files. Explicit sync/network paths remain accepted with a visible unsupported-location warning; no user files move automatically (AM-05).

- **NV-04-01-AC5:** Document/source assertions preserve qualified concepts, units, dimensions and locators; DB/file sinks accept the same validated-batch contract without flattening financial or ETF identities (AM-14/AM-17).

### Verification design

Use actual temporary DuckDB stores for migration/rollback and cross-process lock contention. Insert same content in separate assertion occurrences and incompatible partitions; verify identity separation. Test resolved default/explicit paths and best-effort sync/network diagnostics without treating detection as proof of corruption.

<a id="nv-04-02"></a>
## NV-04-02

**Outcome / scope:** Atomic publication with the three-boundary commit/ack fault matrix, declared platform durability scope, staged checkpoints and duplicate-free retry; prove unchanged polling and A → B → A.

**Requirements:** STORE-01 BUILD-02. **Dependencies:** `NV-04-01` plus applicable prerequisite contracts. Independent work may continue only under the phase dependency rule above.

### Acceptance

- **NV-04-02-AC1:** Failure before commit publishes no facts/lineage/complete coverage; commit with lost acknowledgement and failure after acknowledgement retain the committed state.
- **NV-04-02-AC2:** Retry discovers durable work without duplicates; unchanged polling creates a check while A → B → A and withdrawal/reinstatement preserve distinct admissible occurrences.
- **NV-04-02-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

### Verification design

Inject faults before commit, after commit/before ack and after ack, then restart in a separate process. Assert exact rows/provenance/coverage, not just exceptions; document process-crash versus power-loss durability.

<a id="nv-04-03"></a>
## NV-04-03

**Outcome / scope:** Implement minimal history-bundle/resolved-result snapshots and working export/backup round-trip. Add a tiny synthetic historical-selection proof with original/later/retracted assertions and a missing-price member before adapter breadth; define reproducibility levels without implementing the full Phase 16 engine. Implement atomic direct CSV/Parquet bundles under [§11.6](../specs/storage.md#s11-6); verify no store is opened and schema/provenance/coverage round-trip.

**Requirements:** STORE-02 RES-03. **Dependencies:** `NV-04-02` plus applicable prerequisite contracts. Independent work may continue only under the phase dependency rule above.

### Acceptance

- **NV-04-03-AC1:** History bundles retain admissible candidate versions while resolved results freeze selected outputs; an incomplete snapshot manifest is never published.
- **NV-04-03-AC2:** A tiny temporal example excludes later/retracted assertions at the relevant cutoffs and retains a member with missing price; export/backup restore preserves the declared types and state.
- **NV-04-03-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

- **NV-04-03-AC4:** Direct CSV/Parquet bundles preserve types/provenance/coverage without opening a store. Failure/cancel/disk-full at publication boundaries leaves prior output intact and no complete marker; optional raw omission explicitly limits replay (AM-17).

### Verification design

Use hand-checked timeline fixtures and a real backup→fresh-directory restore. Corrupt/truncate one artifact to prove detection; record ingest/query baselines and logical versus byte-replay limits.
