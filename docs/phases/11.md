<a id="nv-11-01"></a>
## NV-11-01

**Outcome / scope:** Participant OI/volume official reports with category/measure dictionary.

**Requirements:** REP-01. **Dependencies:** Phase prerequisites above and the approved baseline.

### Acceptance

- **NV-11-01-AC1:** Participant OI and volume retain distinct measures, units and category definitions across supported report generations.
- **NV-11-01-AC2:** Parent/subcategory aggregation cannot double-count participants; missing reports and missing categories differ from true zeros.
- **NV-11-01-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

### Verification design

Use small hand-summed report fixtures, category changes and blank/zero measures; assert totals only where the dictionary declares an additive relationship.

## NV-11-02

**Outcome / scope:** Historical bulk/block/short-selling adapters and FII/DII capability-limited acquisition.

**Requirements:** REP-02. **Dependencies:** `NV-11-01` plus applicable prerequisite contracts. Independent work may continue only under the phase dependency rule above.

### Acceptance

- **NV-11-02-AC2:** FII/DII acquisition advertises only measured depth; current-only responses cannot satisfy historical date requests.

## NV-11-03

**Outcome / scope:** Report-level consistency tests, provisional/final revisions, historical depth diagnostics.

**Requirements:** REP-01 REP-02. **Dependencies:** `NV-11-02` plus applicable prerequisite contracts. Independent work may continue only under the phase dependency rule above.

### Acceptance

- **NV-11-03-AC1:** Provisional/final report revisions preserve occurrence and known-at evidence while comparable consistency checks are versioned.

- **NV-11-03-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.
