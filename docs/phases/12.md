<a id="nv-12-01"></a>
## NV-12-01

**Outcome / scope:** Action/event catalogs and typed dates/revisions from permitted sources.

**Requirements:** CORP-01 TIME-01. **Dependencies:** Phase prerequisites above and the approved baseline.

### Acceptance

- **NV-12-01-AC1:** Ex/record/payment/publication dates and precision remain distinct; original/revised action/event catalogs retain occurrence provenance.
- **NV-12-01-AC2:** Missing dates stay unknown and later catalog corrections cannot backdate admissibility.
- **NV-12-01-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.

### Verification design

Use mixed date-only/instant records and revised corporate events; assert timeline selection and field identity without attachment parsing.

## NV-12-02

**Outcome / scope:** Evidence-backed split/bonus factor normalization; unresolved terms retained for review.

**Requirements:** CORP-01. **Dependencies:** `NV-12-01` plus applicable prerequisite contracts. Independent work may continue only under the phase dependency rule above.

### Acceptance

- **NV-12-02-AC1:** Only evidenced split/bonus terms produce factors with an explicit direction and effective date; ambiguous text remains unresolved.
- **NV-12-02-AC2:** Unsupported dividend/rights/merger economic treatment cannot be represented as a validated split factor.
- **NV-12-02-AC3:** Record observed acceptance evidence under [the common evidence rule](../WORKFLOW.md#definition-of-done) for the criteria and verification design below.
